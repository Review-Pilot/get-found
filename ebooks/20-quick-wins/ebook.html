Google Business Profile

Your free shop window on Google Search and Google Maps.

1

Claim and verify your profile

Easy win15 min
  1. 1Search your business name on Google, or go to business.google.com.
  2. 2Click Claim this business (or Add your business) and follow the prompts.
  3. 3Verify using the method Google offers: video, phone, email or postcard.

Why it works: Until it's verified, you can't control how you show up on Google Search and Maps.

Tip: Claimed it years ago? Log in and check the owner email is one you still use.

2

Pick the right main category

Big impact5 min
  1. 1In your profile, choose Edit profile, then Business information.
  2. 2Set your main category to the most specific match, like “Car rental agency”, not “Business”.
  3. 3Add a few extra categories that truly describe what you do.

Why it works: Your main category is one of the biggest signals telling Google which searches you should show up for.

Tip: Only pick categories you really offer. Fewer, accurate ones beat a long list.

3

Make your details perfect

Easy win10 min
  1. 1Use your real business name, exactly as on your signage. No extra keywords.
  2. 2Check your address, phone number and website link.
  3. 3Set your opening hours, plus special hours for public holidays and December.

Why it works: Wrong hours or numbers cost you customers and trust, and Google compares your details with other websites.

Tip: Copy your name, address and phone number once, and paste them exactly the same everywhere else.

Part 1 · Google Business Profile20 Quick Wins
4

Write your business description

Easy win10 min
  1. 1You have up to 750 characters. Put the most important things in the first 250.
  2. 2Say what you do, where you do it and who you help, in plain English.
  3. 3Work in a few natural keywords like “plumber in Claremont”. Don't stuff them or add links.

Why it works: It's your elevator pitch, and it helps Google and AI tools understand what you do.

Tip: Read it out loud. If it sounds like a robot wrote it, rewrite it.

5

Add your services, products and extras

Big impact15 min
  1. 1Add each service you offer, with a one-line description and a price if you can.
  2. 2Add your products if you sell any.
  3. 3Tick every attribute that applies, like “online appointments” or “wheelchair accessible”.

Why it works: Google's AI now answers customer questions using your profile details, so the more complete it is, the better those answers.

Tip: If a detail is true, add it. Every blank field is a missed chance.

6

Add 10 or more real photos

Easy win20 min
  1. 1Take photos on your phone in daylight: outside, inside, your team and your work.
  2. 2Upload them under Photos, and add your logo and a cover photo.
  3. 3Add new photos every week or two.

Why it works: People choose businesses that look real and active, and photos get a lot of attention.

Tip: Skip stock photos. Real beats pretty.

Part 1 · Google Business Profile20 Quick Wins
7

Ask 5 happy customers for a review

Big impact15 min
  1. 1In your profile, click Ask for reviews and copy your review link.
  2. 2WhatsApp it to 5 customers who were happy, with a friendly, personal message.
  3. 3Put a QR code for your review link on your counter or invoices (free tools can make one).

Why it works: Reviews are one of the strongest signals for Google Maps, and AI tools tend to recommend businesses with plenty of good reviews.

Careful: Never pay for reviews or offer rewards for them. It breaks Google's rules.

8

Reply to every review

Easy win10 min
  1. 1Open your reviews and reply to each one, newest first.
  2. 2Thank happy customers by name and mention something specific.
  3. 3For a bad review, stay calm, apologise, and offer to fix it offline.

Why it works: Replies show you care, and customers read them before they choose you.

Tip: Try to reply within 48 hours.

9

Post a weekly update

Easy win10 min
  1. 1In your profile, choose Add update (or Posts).
  2. 2Share an offer, news, a new product or a recent job, with one photo.
  3. 3Add a button such as Call now or Learn more.

Why it works: Fresh posts keep your profile active and give people a reason to click.

Tip: Write four posts in one sitting and you're covered for a month.

Your website

Simple fixes that help Google, AI tools and customers understand you.

10

Put your service and town in your page titles

Big impact15 min
  1. 1In your website's SEO settings, find the page title for each page.
  2. 2Use this pattern: Service in Town | Business name, for example “Plumber in Claremont, Cape Town | Smith Plumbing”.
  3. 3Keep it under about 60 characters, and make every page's title different.

Why it works: The page title is one of the first things Google reads, and it's what people see in search results.

Tip: Use the same words in the big heading (H1) at the top of the page.

11

Match your name, address and phone number

Easy win10 min
  1. 1Put your business name, address and phone number as text in your website footer.
  2. 2Copy them exactly from your Google profile.
  3. 3Add a Google Map to your contact page.

Why it works: When your details match everywhere, Google and AI tools can trust you're a real, stable business.

Tip: Use text, not an image. Google can't read words inside pictures.

12

Make it easy to call or WhatsApp

Easy win15 min
  1. 1Add a Call now button that opens the phone dialler (a link that starts with tel:).
  2. 2Add a WhatsApp button (a link like wa.me/27741234567, your number with country code, no spaces or plus).
  3. 3Check on your own phone that both are easy to tap near the top of the page.

Why it works: Most local searches happen on phones. Make contacting you a one-tap job.

Tip: Many South African customers would rather WhatsApp than call.

Part 2 · Your website20 Quick Wins
13

Make your site fast on a phone

Easy win20 min
  1. 1Go to pagespeed.web.dev and test your home page.
  2. 2Shrink big photos with a free tool like Squoosh or TinyPNG before you upload them.
  3. 3Remove what you don't need, like old plug-ins and auto-playing videos.

Why it works: Slow pages lose visitors, and speed is part of how Google judges your site.

Tip: Aim for photos under about 200 KB each.

14

Give each service its own page

Big impact45 min
  1. 1Make a separate page for each main service (and each main area you serve).
  2. 2On each one, explain in your own words what you do, who it's for, your prices or process, and add real photos.
  3. 3Add a button to call, WhatsApp or book.

Why it works: One page per service gives Google, and your customers, a clear answer for each search.

Tip: Start with the service that earns you the most money.

15

Connect Google Search Console

Easy win15 min
  1. 1Go to search.google.com/search-console and sign in with your Google account.
  2. 2Add your website and verify it. The page walks you through it.
  3. 3Submit your sitemap, usually yourwebsite.com/sitemap.xml.

Why it works: It's free, it shows how people find you, and it tells you if Google has trouble with your site.

Tip: Check back once a month to see which searches bring people to you.

AI search

Be the business ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity talk about.

16

Test yourself in AI search

Easy win10 min
  1. 1Open ChatGPT, Gemini or Perplexity.
  2. 2Ask: “Who is the best [your service] in [your town]?” and “Tell me about [your business name].”
  3. 3Screenshot what each says: who gets recommended, and what they say about you (right or wrong).

Why it works: Now you know where you stand today and what to fix first.

Tip: Answers change between tools and from day to day. Test a few times and look for patterns.

17

Answer your customers' questions on your website

Big impact30 min
  1. 1Write down the 5 to 8 questions customers ask you most.
  2. 2Add a Frequently Asked Questions section to your site, or to the right service page.
  3. 3Answer each in 2 or 3 plain sentences, with the direct answer first.

Why it works: AI tools love clear, direct answers, and so do your customers.

Tip: Use your customers' own words, not industry jargon.

18

List your business in more places

Easy win30 min
  1. 1Claim your free profiles on Bing Places and Apple Business Connect.
  2. 2Set up a Facebook business page and a LinkedIn page if you don't have them.
  3. 3Add your business to 2 or 3 directories that suit your industry and area, using exactly the same name, address and phone number each time.

Why it works: AI tools and search engines gather information from many places, and matching details build trust.

Tip: Keep a note of where you're listed so you can update everything at once.

Part 3 · AI search20 Quick Wins
19

Get mentioned by other local sites

Big impact30 min
  1. 1List 5 local businesses, suppliers, associations or community sites you already know.
  2. 2Ask each one to mention or link to your business, for example on a “partners” page or in a newsletter.
  3. 3Say yes to local events, podcasts and write-ups, and link to them from your site.

Why it works: Mentions from trusted local sources are strong proof that you're a real, respected business in your area.

Tip: Offer something back: a quote, a useful tip or a link to them.

20

Make it a 15-minute monthly habit

Easy win15 min / month
  1. 1Once a month, reply to new reviews and ask 2 or 3 customers for a fresh one.
  2. 2Add a post and a few new photos, and check your hours and details.
  3. 3Re-run your AI test from Win 16 and see what's changed.

Why it works: Steady, regular activity beats one big burst. Local SEO is a habit, not a one-off job.

Tip: Put a repeating reminder in your phone calendar right now.

How AI tools pick who to recommend (in plain English)

Strong Google Maps presence

Businesses that show up well on Maps tend to be recommended more often.

Plenty of good reviews

Lots of recent, positive reviews are a powerful signal.

Matching details everywhere

The same name, address and phone number across the web builds trust.

Mentions by trusted sites

Local sites, directories and news pieces that name you add proof.

Clear answers on your site

Plain, direct answers to real customer questions are easy for AI to use.

AI answers change from day to day and between tools, so no one can control them. But doing these things well puts you in the best position.
